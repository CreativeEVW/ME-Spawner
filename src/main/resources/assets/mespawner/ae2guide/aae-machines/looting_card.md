# Looting Card

<ItemImage id="looting_card" scale="8" />

Each Looting Card doubles the quantity of drops produced.

- **Max Installed**: 3
- **Slot**: ME Spawner upgrade slots 2-4
- Effects stack exponentially: 1 card = ×2, 2 cards = ×4, 3 cards = ×8
