# Probability Card

<ItemImage id="probability_card" scale="8" />

When installed, iterates the loot table and generates 1 of every possible drop.

- **Max installs**: 1
- **Applies to**: ME Spawner upgrade slot 1 (not applicable to the Spawner Controller)
