# Experience Crystal

<ItemImage id="experience_crystal" scale="8" />

Produced from products destroyed by the [Spawner Controller](spawner_controller.md).

## Experience Crystal

- **Right-click**: grants the same experience as a bottle o' enchanting (3-11 points)
- **Shift-right-click**: absorbs the entire held stack at once
- Crafted with a glass bottle into a bottle o' enchanting

## Experience Crystal Block

<ItemImage id="experience_crystal_block" scale="8" />

Crafted from 9 experience crystals. **Cannot be placed**, only used.

- **Right-click**: grants 1 experience level
- **Shift-right-click**: consumes the whole held stack (1 level per block)
