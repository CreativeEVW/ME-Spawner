# Looting Card Family

<ItemImage id="looting_card" scale="4" />
<ItemImage id="compressed_looting_card" scale="4" />
<ItemImage id="ultimate_looting_card" scale="4" />

Multiplies drop counts. The three tiers can be mixed; effects multiply.

| Type | Recipe | Multiplier |
|------|--------|------------|
| Looting card | - | x2 |
| Compressed looting card | 9x looting cards | x6 |
| Ultimate looting card | 9x compressed looting cards | x9 |

- **Max installs**: up to 3 per tier, but only the top 3 multipliers count
- **Applies to**: ME Spawner upgrade slots 2-4; Spawner Controller upgrade slots 4-6 (on the controller it scales ALL products, including converted experience crystals)
